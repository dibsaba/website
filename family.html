Severe Behavior Consultation

When a child engages in dangerous behaviors (aggression, self-injury), standard ABA is often not enough. We provide high-level functional analysis and safety planning, collaborating with any service providers you currently have.

  • Functional Behavior Assessment (FBA)
  • Safety Care & De-Escalation
  • Collaboration with all Providers

Independent Educational Evaluations

If you disagree with the evaluation provided by your school district, you have the right to an IEE. We provide unbiased, thorough evaluations to determine if your child is receiving the support they are legally entitled to, and advocate for your child at IEP meetings.

Advanced Parent Training

We move beyond "basics" and teach parents how to be the therapeutic agent in their own home. Ideally suited for families on waitlists, those who have been denied services due to extreme behaviors, and those who want to supplement services they already have.
