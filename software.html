Engineered for Learning Solutions

Generic software failed to handle the complexity of Learning Solutions' multi-site operations. So we built a custom engine that handles Hard Constraints (non-negotiables) and Soft Objectives (optimizations) simultaneously.

Burnout Patterns

The solver identifies specific sequences (e.g., High-High-High severity sessions) and mathematically prevents them from being assigned to staff.

Fairness & Equity

Powered by Google OR-Tools, our Leximin approach ensures no single technician carries an unfair burden. It mathematically smooths out the difficult sessions across your entire team.

Tiered Priorities

It solves "Must-Haves" first, locks them in, then solves "Nice-to-Haves." This ensures compliance is never sacrificed for convenience.

Equitable Optimization

Built on the Google OR-Tools engine, the Strategy Editor allows you to define your center's logic.

It uses a technique called Leximin Optimization to guarantee equitable workloads—lifting the bottom to match the top—so everyone is treated fairly.
